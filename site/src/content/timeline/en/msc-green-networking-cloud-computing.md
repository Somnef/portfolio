A second Master's degree, funded by an Erasmus Mundus scholarship and taught
across three countries — a semester in France, a semester in the United
Kingdom, and a semester in Sweden — with the final months spent on the thesis
project in France.

The specialisation is green networking and cloud computing: building and
operating distributed systems with their energy cost treated as a design
constraint rather than an afterthought.

This is where most of the cloud and infrastructure work took shape, including
the Terraform and Kubernetes projects.

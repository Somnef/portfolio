A side project with a group of friends: find Algerian data — historical,
societal, economic — mine it with scrapers because very little of it is
published in any usable form, then present it visually.

The scraping was the unglamorous majority of the work. The payoff was a set of
animations and visualisations that made trends legible to people who would
never open a spreadsheet.
